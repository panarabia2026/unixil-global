About Unixil

Your local partner with global outreach

Unixil Global FZCO is headquartered in Dubai, with presence in the UAE, USA, India and China.

Our story

We offer clients not just products but a partnership — one in which we are committed to their success.

Unixil Global brings together industrial technical solutions across instrumentation, electrical, telecom and mechanical disciplines — including GRP cable management systems and junction boxes for hazardous areas.

We use a collaborative technical process to understand each client's needs and tailor a solution that delivers results. Our seamless, well-integrated supply chain lets us deliver reliably, responsibly and efficiently to customers around the world.
